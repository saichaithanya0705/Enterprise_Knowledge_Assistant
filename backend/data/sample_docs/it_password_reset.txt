IT SUPPORT GUIDE: PASSWORD & ACCOUNT ACCESS

Resetting Your Password
1. Go to the self-service portal at portal.company.internal/reset.
2. Enter your company email address.
3. You will receive a one-time verification code via SMS to your registered phone number. Enter the code within 10 minutes.
4. Choose a new password that meets the complexity requirements: minimum 12 characters, at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
5. Your new password takes effect immediately across all company systems (email, VPN, internal tools).

If You Are Locked Out
Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts within 15 minutes. Locked accounts automatically unlock after 30 minutes, or you may contact the IT Helpdesk for an immediate unlock by calling extension 4357 (HELP) or opening a ticket at helpdesk.company.internal.

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
All employees are required to enable 2FA on their company account within their first week. Supported methods are the Company Authenticator app (recommended) or SMS. To set up 2FA, go to Account Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication and follow the prompts. If you lose your 2FA device, contact IT Security immediately to have it deregistered.

VPN Access
Remote employees must connect via the company VPN client before accessing internal systems. VPN credentials are the same as your company login. If the VPN client fails to connect, first check that you are on a stable internet connection, then restart the client. Persistent issues should be logged with IT Helpdesk with the error code shown.

Requesting New Hardware or Software
Hardware and software requests go through the IT Service Catalog. Standard requests (laptop accessories, common software licenses) are typically fulfilled within 3 business days. Non-standard requests require manager approval and may take up to 2 weeks.

Contact
IT Helpdesk: helpdesk.company.internal or extension 4357, available Monday-Friday 8am-7pm.
