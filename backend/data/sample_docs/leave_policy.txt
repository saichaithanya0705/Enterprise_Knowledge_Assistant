EMPLOYEE LEAVE POLICY

Purpose
This policy explains the types of leave available to full-time employees of the company and how to request them.

Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 18 days of annual leave per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per completed month of service. Unused annual leave up to 10 days may be carried forward into the next calendar year; any balance beyond that is forfeited on December 31st. Annual leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance and require manager approval.

Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences longer than 2 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal within 3 working days of returning to work. Sick leave does not carry forward and resets each January 1st.

Casual Leave
Employees may take up to 6 casual leave days per year for personal matters that do not qualify as sick leave. Casual leave requests should be submitted at least 1 working day in advance where possible.

Maternity and Paternity Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption. Requests should be submitted to HR at least 30 days before the expected leave start date, along with supporting medical documentation.

Bereavement Leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling), and up to 2 days for extended family.

Unpaid Leave
Unpaid leave of up to 30 days may be granted at management discretion for extenuating circumstances, subject to HR approval and continued benefits eligibility review.

How to Apply
All leave requests, regardless of type, must be submitted through the HR portal under "My Leave > New Request." Verbal or email-only requests are not considered official until logged in the system.

Contact
For questions not covered here, contact the HR team at hr-support@company.internal.
