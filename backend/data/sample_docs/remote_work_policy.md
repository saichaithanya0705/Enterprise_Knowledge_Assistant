# Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## Eligibility
Employees whose roles do not require regular on-site presence (as determined by their department head) are eligible for hybrid work, defined as a minimum of 2 days per week in the office. Fully remote arrangements require Director-level approval and are reviewed every 6 months.

## Core Hours
Regardless of work location, employees are expected to be available and responsive between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM in their local time zone, unless otherwise agreed with their manager.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a one-time $300 home office stipend for eligible remote/hybrid employees, covering items such as a monitor, chair, or desk. Requests are submitted through the IT Service Catalog under "Home Office Equipment."

## Security Requirements
Remote work requires: company VPN for all internal system access, full-disk encryption enabled on all company devices, and screen lock set to activate after 5 minutes of inactivity. Working from public Wi-Fi without VPN is prohibited for accessing internal systems.

## Time Zone & Travel
Employees working remotely from a different time zone than their team for more than 2 consecutive weeks must notify their manager and HR in advance, as this may affect tax and compliance obligations.

## Performance Expectations
Remote and hybrid work does not change performance expectations. Managers and employees should agree on clear deliverables and check-in cadence as part of regular 1:1s.
