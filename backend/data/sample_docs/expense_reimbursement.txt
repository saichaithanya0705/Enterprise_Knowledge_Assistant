EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Purpose
This document describes what business expenses are reimbursable and how to submit a claim.

Eligible Expenses
The following categories are eligible for reimbursement when incurred for legitimate business purposes: travel (flights, trains, taxis/rideshare), lodging, client meals (up to $75 per person per meal), internet/phone charges for remote work (up to $40/month), conference and training fees pre-approved by a manager, and office supplies under $50 without prior approval.

Non-Reimbursable Expenses
Alcohol (except at approved client events), personal entertainment, traffic fines, first-class flight upgrades, and expenses without an original receipt are not reimbursable.

Submission Process
1. Log the expense in the Expense Portal within 30 days of the purchase date.
2. Attach a clear photo or scan of the original receipt. Claims without receipts over $25 will be rejected.
3. Select the correct cost center and expense category.
4. Submit for manager approval.
5. Approved claims are paid out in the next payroll cycle, typically within 10-15 business days.

Mileage Reimbursement
Personal vehicle use for business travel is reimbursed at $0.35 per mile. Commuting between home and the primary office is not reimbursable.

Advance Requests
For anticipated expenses over $500 (e.g. international travel), employees may request a cash advance through the Expense Portal at least 10 business days before travel. Advances must be reconciled with actual receipts within 5 business days of return.

Approval Limits
Managers may approve claims up to $1,000. Claims above $1,000 require Finance Director approval in addition to manager sign-off.

Contact
Questions about reimbursement should go to finance-support@company.internal.
